1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lunao currently provides three high-ticket client acquisition campaign types:
  1. Site Deploy Campaign    (Cloudflare worker automated multi-site synthesis)
  2. Email Campaign          (Multi-inbox warmup, OAuth Gmail/Outlook, Dork/CSV)
  3. SMS Campaign            (Telnyx 10DLC automated SMS proposal outreach)

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Currently, the Email Campaign Wizard (EmailCampaignWizard.tsx) has achieved 
a state of high visual polish: luxury dark/light brutalist typography, 
smooth spring animations, glowing stepper track, interactive card grids, 
rich audio triggers, and unified progress/completion overlays.
However, Site Deploy (embedded inside Campaigns.tsx) and SMS Campaign 
(SmsCampaignWizard.tsx) use older, inconsistent UI designs:
  - Site Deploy uses small, plain buttons for niche selection, a bare-bones
    dashed box for CSV upload, a cramped template grid, and lacks the 
    modern progress toggle and celebration screen.
  - SMS Campaign uses a different stepper style, mismatched tab pill headers,
    different dropzone borders, and inconsistently styled phone number pickers.

MISSION:
Unify all three campaign flows to be 100% VISUALLY IDENTICAL, COHESIVE, AND 
SHARED IN DESIGN VOCABULARY. When switching between Site Deploy, Email, and 
SMS, the user will experience the exact same design language, card layouts, 
button micro-interactions, responsive horizontal steppers, audio soundscapes, 
and celebration screen architecture.

3. THE GOLD STANDARD: EMAIL CAMPAIGN DESIGN TOKENS & UI PATTERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To achieve 100% identical styling, all three wizards must strictly adhere to 
these exact CSS classes, Tailwind patterns, and micro-interaction mechanics:

3.1 Color Palette & Surfaces:
  - Surface Background:       bg-off-white (#F7F6F3) / bg-white
  - Primary Brand Accent:     text-accent (#2563EB / Royal Blue), bg-accent, bg-accent-soft
  - Border Lines:             border-border-main (#E5E2DC), border-border-light (#EFECE6)
  - Typography Colors:        text-ink (#1A1916), text-ink-secondary (#6E6A63), text-ink-tertiary (#A29E96)
  - Success Green:            text-success (#059669), bg-success-soft (#ECFDF5)
  - Alert / Danger Red:       text-danger (#DC2626), bg-danger-soft (#FEF2F2)

3.2 Typography Hierarchy:
  - Step Titles:              font-serif text-2xl text-ink leading-tight
  - Step Subtitles:           text-sm text-ink-secondary leading-relaxed
  - Section Micro-Labels:     text-[11px] font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-ink-secondary
  - Interactive Action Chips: text-xs font-semibold font-sans

3.3 Animation & Sound Standard:
  - Tab Switch / Step Next:   playGentleChime(stepNumber) + playSoftTap()
  - Option Selection:         playSoftBubble() or playElegantBell()
  - Launch Swell:             playLaunchSwell()
  - Final Celebration:        playVictoryCelebration()
  - Error Tone:               playElegantError()
  - Container Transitions:    animate-fade-in duration-300 ease-out
  - Selected Card Pop:        animate-niche-card-pop scale-[1.02]

4. STEP-BY-STEP UNIFICATION MATRIX FOR ALL THREE CAMPAIGNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every campaign wizard will follow a unified 5-STEP workflow structure.

================================================================================
STEP 1: SELECT NICHE (100% IDENTICAL ACROSS ALL THREE)
================================================================================
* Title:      "Select your niche"
* Subtitle:   "This determines which website templates will be available. Tap a card to select."
* Visuals:    4-column responsive grid (grid-cols-2 sm:grid-cols-3 lg:grid-cols-4 gap-3 sm:gap-4)
* Card Design:
  - Outer:    rounded-2xl border transition-all duration-150 cursor-pointer overflow-hidden
  - Active:   bg-gradient-to-br from-accent-soft via-white to-accent-soft/60 border-accent/40 
              ring-4 ring-accent/20 shadow-[0_18px_40px_rgba(99,102,241,0.18)]
  - Inactive: bg-white border-border-main hover:border-accent/30 hover:shadow-lg hover:-translate-y-1
  - Squircle: w-14 h-14 sm:w-16 sm:h-16 rounded-2xl flex items-center justify-center text-3xl
              (Active rotates -6deg with drop shadow)
  - Status:   "Selected" with check icon vs "Tap to select" in text-ink-tertiary
* Behavior:   Clicking any niche immediately triggers playSoftBubble(), updates the 
              selectedNiche state, updates campaign default name, and enables "Next Step".

================================================================================
STEP 2: LEAD SOURCE & BUSINESSES (SHARED SHELL WITH CONTEXTUAL TABS)
================================================================================
* Header:     "Choose your leads"
* Subtitle:   Contextual description based on campaign type.
* Tab Pill Bar:
  - Container: flex gap-2 p-1.5 bg-gradient-to-r from-surface to-off-white rounded-2xl w-fit shadow-inner
  - Active Pill: bg-white text-accent shadow-lg shadow-accent/20 ring-2 ring-accent/30
  - Inactive Pill: text-ink-secondary hover:text-ink hover:bg-white/50

* Tab Variants per Campaign:
  A. Site Deploy:
     - Tab 1: "Upload CSV" (Primary) — accepts business_name, city, phone_number, etc.
     - Tab 2: "Quick Lead Generator" — generate realistic local business leads for instant testing.
  B. SMS Campaign:
     - Tab 1: "Upload CSV" (Primary) — accepts business_name, phone_number, city.
     - Tab 2: "Find Leads in Canada" (Google Places discovery tool with Canada filter).
  C. Email Campaign:
     - Tab 1: "Auto Email Sender" (Google Dork engine).
     - Tab 2: "Upload CSV" (Email list).

* CSV Upload Experience (100% IDENTICAL FOR ALL THREE):
  - Dropzone: Luxury large rounded-2xl border-2 border-dashed border-border-main
              Decorative backdrop radial blur gradient.
              Sparkle icon with subtle bounce animation.
              "Drop your CSV file here" / "or click to browse from your computer".
              Accent colored "Choose File" button.
  - Preview State: Uses the shared CsvPreview component showing parsed count,
                   columns detected, preview table, and "Upload another CSV" action.

================================================================================
STEP 3: CHOOSE TEMPLATE (100% IDENTICAL ACROSS ALL THREE)
================================================================================
* Hero Banner Card:
  - Rounded-2xl bg-gradient-to-br from-accent/5 via-white to-accent/10 border border-accent/20
  - Globe icon inside glowing badge with animated ping ring.
  - Title: "Pick your template"
  - Subtitle: "Each site will be personalized with the lead's business info. Click any card for a full preview."
  - Badges: "[X] templates available" + "[Niche] niche selected"
* Template Grid:
  - 3-column responsive layout (grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-3 gap-6 md:gap-8)
  - Large aspect-[16/11] TemplateSimPreview container.
  - Hover overlay with glassmorphism "Preview" button (triggers TemplatePreviewModal).
  - Selected state: border-accent shadow-2xl ring-4 ring-accent/10 scale-[1.02] with pulse glow.
  - Badges on preview: Niche tag top-left, selection circle top-right, "Most Popular" star bottom-left.

================================================================================
STEP 4: CONNECTION & DISPATCH INFRASTRUCTURE
================================================================================
* Contextual setup step tailored to each campaign's underlying protocol:

  A. Email Campaign:
     - Connected Inboxes (Gmail / Outlook OAuth accounts list).
     - Account probe cards showing warmup status, send limit, and reconnect button.

  B. SMS Campaign:
     - Phone Number & Telnyx Profile selection (SmsAccountStep).
     - Redesigned into the same luxury card style:
       * Dedicated "Selected Sender Number" card with signal strength badge.
       * Automated provisioning status for Free/Starter plans.
       * 10DLC registration compliance badge.

  C. Site Deploy Campaign:
     - Deployment Target & Domain Routing Configuration:
       * Cloudflare Edge Deployment Engine card with active status badge.
       * Subdomain preview banner: `https://[business-slug].lunao.app`
       * Instant preview switch: "Enable Instant AI Landing Page Customizer"
       * Zero external credentials required — automatic edge proxying.

================================================================================
STEP 5: REVIEW, CONTENT CUSTOMIZATION & LAUNCH
================================================================================
* Unified Layout Structure:
  - Left Column (7/12 width): Message editor / deployment payload inspector with tag chips.
  - Right Column (5/12 width): Device Simulator preview (Desktop/Mobile toggle) + Cost Summary.

* Left Column Variants:
  A. Email Campaign: Subject input + Body textarea + Dynamic variable chips (`{{business_name}}`, `{{city}}`, `{{site_url}}`).
  B. SMS Campaign: Message textarea + Variable chips + Live character counter + 160-char SMS segment indicator.
  C. Site Deploy Campaign: Business payload review list + Automated site meta configuration + SEO title & OpenGraph card summary.

* Right Column: Cost Summary & Launch Card (IDENTICAL FORMAT):
  - Cost per unit badge (Site Deploy: 1 credit | Email: 2 credits | SMS: 2-3 credits).
  - Formula display: "[X] Leads × [Y] Credits = [Total] Credits Required".
  - Current User Balance with sufficiency check.
  - Big primary launch button:
    * `Launch Site Deploy Campaign` (Globe icon)
    * `Launch Email Outreach` (Mail/Send icon)
    * `Launch SMS Outreach` (MessageSquare icon)

================================================================================
POST-LAUNCH: PROGRESS TOGGLE & CELEBRATION (IDENTICAL ARCHITECTURE)
================================================================================
1. Floating Progress Bar (`CampaignProgressToggle`):
   - Minimized floating bar docked at the bottom right.
   - Shows live counters: Sites Staged, Dispatches Sent, Failures, Percentage.
   - Expandable into full progress modal.
2. Victory Celebration Screen:
   - Green & white luxury celebration container with CheckCircle icon.
   - 4-card metric counter pop (Total Leads, Sites Built, Sent, Failed).
   - Live deployed site links preview drawer.
   - "Launch another campaign" + "View in Recent Campaigns" action buttons.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. RESILIENCE, ERROR HANDLING & OFFLINE SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In accordance with AI Studio guidelines and the Lunao architectural standards:
  1. No unhandled Promise rejections on missing Express :8787 endpoints.
  2. Automatic optimistic fallback to `src/data/mockData.ts` when offline.
  3. Strict client-side CSV validation (missing columns highlighted in red pills,
     country detection, phone format normalization).
  4. Real-time credit quota verification (blocks launch button with clear, polite
     insufficient credit callout and link to Plans tab).
  5. One-line error reporting in chat and modal toasts without expanding or breaking
     surrounding layout containers.
