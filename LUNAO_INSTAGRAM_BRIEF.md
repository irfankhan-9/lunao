## BRAND IDENTITY

### The Vibe
**"Premium Editorial Catalog Meets Trusted SaaS."** Lunao's aesthetic is calm, authoritative, and beautifully spare — like a high-end print magazine that also happens to be a piece of software. It is NOT startup-flashy. It is NOT dark-mode-techy. It is warm cream, deep ink, and sharp blue. Think *Kinfolk* magazine x Stripe's documentation.

### Color Palette (Exact Hex Codes)

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Warm Cream | `#F8F7F5` |
| Surface | Light Stone | `#F2F1EE` |
| Border | Soft Taupe | `#E4E2DC` |
| Ink (Headings) | Near Black | `#1A1916` |
| Ink Secondary | Warm Gray | `#6B6960` |
| Ink Tertiary | Muted Taupe | `#A8A4A0` |
| Accent (CTAs, Highlights) | Royal Blue | `#2563EB` |
| Accent Hover | Deep Blue | `#1D4ED8` |
| Accent Soft | Blue Tint | `#EFF3FF` |
| Success | Forest Green | `#16A34A` |
| Danger | Crimson | `#DC2626` |

### Typography

| Element | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Headlines / Big Claims** | **Instrument Serif** (Google Fonts) | Regular (400) or Italic | Use for 1-3 word emotional punch lines. Never all-caps. |
| **Body / Descriptions** | **DM Sans** (Google Fonts) | Regular (400) or Medium (500) | Clean, readable, slightly warm. Not Inter. |
| **Labels / Badges / Eyebrows** | **JetBrains Mono** (Google Fonts) | Medium or Bold | ALL CAPS with letter-spacing: 0.12em. Used for small tag-style labels. |

### Typography Rules
- Headline text: **Instrument Serif**, 24-40px, `#1A1916`, tight tracking
- Subtext: **DM Sans**, 13-15px, `#6B6960`
- Labels/tags: **JetBrains Mono**, 10-11px, ALL CAPS, `#A8A4A0`, letter-spacing 0.12em
- CTA text: **DM Sans**, Medium weight, `#2563EB` or white on blue

### Layout Principles
- **Generous whitespace.** Never crowd the canvas. Let elements breathe.
- **One idea per slide.** Don't try to say everything at once.
- **Strong visual hierarchy:** big serif headline -> small body -> tiny mono label.
- **Use borders sparingly.** Thin 1px `#E4E2DC` borders on cards give structure without weight.
- **Rounded corners:** Use `border-radius: 12-16px` on cards and containers.
- **Bottom-aligned CTAs** when there's a call to action. Keep the top half for impact.

## VOICE & TONE RULES

### Say This
- Direct, specific, factual
- Short sentences. No filler.
- "Four credits per lead." Not "Our platform helps you maximize your lead generation efficiency."
- "You pick Barber + Austin, TX. You get 60 leads in 40 seconds." Not "Our intelligent matching system leverages location signals to surface high-intent prospects."
- Numbers and specifics beat vague promises every time.
- Use dollar amounts and time estimates as weapons. "2 hours per mockup" is more powerful than "slow workflow."
- Make the pain visceral. The agency owner should feel recognized when they read it.

### Do NOT Say This
- No "leverage," "synergy," "game-changer," "cutting-edge," "revolutionize"
- No "seamless" or "robust"
- No emoji in the copy
- No ALL CAPS headlines (only use ALL CAPS for labels/tags in JetBrains Mono)
- Avoid exclamation marks — periods are more authoritative
