This file contains every feature, capability, and value proposition of Lunao.
Use this as the master context file when planning social media content,
writing Instagram captions, or feeding to an LLM before creating posts.

Lunao is NOT just a website builder — it is a full client acquisition,
retention, and scaling system for local service agencies.

The product has three major pillars:
  1. FIND clients (lead discovery + outreach)
  2. WIN clients (instant website generation + SMS pitching)
  3. KEEP clients (mobile app, bookings, chatbot, site editing, scaling tools)

Every feature below plays an equally important role in this system.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  THE CORE PROBLEM LUNAO SOLVES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Local digital marketing agencies waste countless hours doing three painful
things manually:

  1. FINDING leads — Scrolling through Google Maps one by one, copying phone
     numbers into spreadsheets, trying to figure out which businesses don't
     have websites or have bad ones.

  2. PITCHING leads — Manually building demo websites for each prospect,
     writing personalized messages, sending texts one by one, tracking who
     replied and who didn't.

  3. RETAINING clients — After landing the client, juggling emails, calls,
     missed bookings, no way for the client to manage their own site, no
     chatbot, no booking system. Client churns in 3 months.

Lunao solves all three in one dashboard. Find → Win → Keep.

The result: agencies go from closing 2-3 clients a month (with 40+ hours of
manual work) to closing 10-50+ clients a month with a fraction of the effort.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FEATURE 1: LEAD DISCOVERY & TARGETING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What it does:
  - Agencies pick a niche (Barber, Salon, Dentist, HVAC, Gym, Roofing,
    Real Estate — or any local service) and a city.
  - Lunao scans local business databases and surfaces high-intent prospects:
    businesses that don't have a website, have a bad one, or aren't
    optimized for mobile.
  - Each lead comes with the business name, owner, phone number, address,
    Google rating, review count, and current website status.

Pain point it solves:
  - No more spending 3 hours on Google Maps per city copying business info
    into a spreadsheet. Lunao does it in seconds.
  - Only surfaces businesses that NEED help — not ones that already have
    great websites. Every lead is pre-qualified.

Why agencies love it:
  - They can target specific cities and provinces — from Toronto to Austin
    to Vancouver. Full geographic control.
  - They can see exactly how many prospects are available before spending
    any resources.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FEATURE 2: INSTANT WEBSITE GENERATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What it does:
  - Lunao generates a complete, premium, fully responsive website for any
    local business in under 30 seconds.
  - The site is personalized with the business's real name, city, phone
    number, address, Google rating, reviews — everything pulled from the
    lead data.
  - 8 built-in industry-specific premium templates: Barber, Salon, Dentist,
    HVAC, Gym, Roofing, Real Estate, and a second Barber variant.
  - Each template is hand-designed with luxury aesthetics: custom Google
    Fonts, niche-appropriate color palettes, smooth animations, full mobile
    responsiveness, and professional copywriting.

Pain point it solves:
  - Agencies no longer need to hire a designer or spend hours in WordPress
    building a demo site for each prospect. Lunao builds it automatically.
  - The site looks so polished that prospects think the agency spent days
    designing it — but it took 30 seconds.

Why agencies love it:
  - They can generate 50 unique, personalized websites in one campaign run
    and have them all live on the internet immediately.
  - Every site is deployed on a global edge CDN (Cloudflare Pages) with
    instant SSL — loads blazing fast on any phone.

  FEATURE 3: TEMPLATE LAB — AI-POWERED CUSTOM TEMPLATES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What it does:

  - Describe what you want in plain English: "A luxury dental clinic with

Pain point it solves:

Why agencies love it:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FEATURE 4: SMS OUTREACH & AUTOMATED CAMPAIGNS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What it does:
  - After generating websites for a batch of prospects, Lunao automatically
    sends a personalized SMS to each business owner with a link to their
    custom website.
  - The message is fully customizable with dynamic tokens: the business
    name, city, and live site URL are auto-inserted for each recipient.
  - Example pitch: "Hi, we noticed {{business_name}} doesn't have a
    website yet to showcase your services in {{city}}. We built a custom
    preview for you — take a look: {{site_url}}. Reply YES to publish!"
  - Real SMS delivery through professional telecom infrastructure.
  - Delivery tracking: every message is tracked — sent, delivered, failed,
    or replied.
  - Automatic refunds: if a message fails to send (bad number, opt-out),
    the credits are automatically refunded.

Pain point it solves:
  - No more copying phone numbers and sending texts one by one from your
    personal phone.
  - No more guessing whether the message was delivered — full transparency.
  - No wasted money: failed sends = automatic refund.

Why agencies love it:
  - They can pitch 50 businesses in one click with personalized messages
    and live website links. Response rates are dramatically higher than
    cold emails because the prospect sees a live site built JUST for them.

Why agencies love it:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

What it does:

Pain point it solves:
  - No need to know HTML, CSS, or use WordPress. Anyone can edit a live

Why agencies love it:
