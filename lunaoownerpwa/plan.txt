                    LUNAO BUSINESS SUITE - MASTER PRODUCT PLAN
                  End-to-End Architecture & Mobile UI System

1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Primary Goal: Provide business owners with an ultra-responsive, zero-friction 
  management suite for automated front-desk operations, appointments, clients,
  automated communications, and live website publishing.
• Visual Identity: Clean, high-contrast white & emerald green design system 
  (warm slate neutrals, emerald-600/700 primary accents, slate-900 typography).
• Mobile-First Ergonomics: Every view is optimized for one-thumb mobile navigation
  with minimum 44px touch targets, fixed bottom navigation, collapsible drawers,
  swipeable bottom sheets, and responsive grid reflows.
• Tactile Sensory Feedback:
  - Instant auto-scroll-to-top whenever switching sections or entering the app.
  - Relaxing, non-intrusive harmonic chimes (528Hz Solfeggio tones) for section changes.
  - Micro-haptic tactile clicks on button taps and tab switches.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TENANT AUTHENTICATION & ACCESS CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Owner Access Code Gate (OwnerAuthModal):
  - 4-8 character uppercase owner code authentication (e.g. BARBER01, DENTAL02, GLOW03).
  - Pre-seeded test credentials with 1-click quick login pills for immediate audit.
  - Instant session persistence in localStorage with graceful fallback to default tenant.
  - Auto-scrolls to top upon successful verification.
• Tenant Switcher (TopHeader & Settings):
  - Fast switcher dropdown in top header on desktop.
  - Full tenant management card in Settings and mobile More Drawer.
  - Dynamically synchronizes all underlying state (bookings, customers, transcripts,
    email logs, and AI receptionist personality).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. APP SHELL & RESPONSIVE NAVIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Desktop Sidebar (w-64):
  - Persistent left rail on lg+ viewports.
  - Active section indicator with emerald background pill and left accent glow.
  - Section count badges (e.g. active bookings, total clients).
  - Business monogram badge and live tenant status card at the bottom.
• Mobile Top Header:
  - Slim 56px sticky header with business logo/monogram, tenant switcher,
    PWA install badge, and notification center bell.
• Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar (MobileNav):
  - Fixed bottom bar (z-40) with 4 primary action tabs (Dashboard, Bookings, Clients, Inquiries)
    plus a "More" drawer trigger.
  - Main content padding-bottom (`pb-24`) ensures no button is ever obstructed by the nav bar.
• Mobile "More" Drawer (MoreDrawer):
  - Slide-over sheet giving instant access to secondary modules: AI Receptionist,
    Website Builder, Automations, Email Center, Settings, Switch Tenant, and Logout.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DETAILED SECTION BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[SECTION 1: DASHBOARD (DashboardPage)]
• Mobile Layout: Single-column vertical stack with responsive stat cards.
• Metric StatCards:
  - Today's Appointments (Live count of confirmed bookings scheduled for today).
  - Upcoming Bookings (Confirmed appointments this week).
  - Total Clients (Verified client profiles in directory).
• Quick Launch Tiles:
  - Record New Appointment (opens New Booking BottomSheet).
  - Send Email Outreach (navigates to Email Center).
  - Configure AI Agent (navigates to AI Receptionist settings).
  - Preview Live Website (navigates to Website Builder).
• Live Appointments Feed:
  - Filterable date list showing customer name, service, time slot, and status badge.
• Real-Time Activity Feed:
  - Chronological audit log of bookings created, messages received, and emails sent.

[SECTION 2: BOOKINGS & CALENDAR (BookingsPage)]
• View Modes:
  - List View: Chronological list of bookings with search and status filter chips.
  - Calendar View: 7-Day responsive schedule grid with time-slot blocks.
• Strict Status Lifecycle (No 'pending' state):
  - 'confirmed' (Active confirmed appointment with green badge).
  - 'completed' (Service delivered with blue/accent badge).
  - 'cancelled' (Cancelled appointment with neutral slate badge).
• Filter Chips: 'All', 'Confirmed', 'Completed', 'Cancelled'.
• Appointment Details BottomSheet:
  - Client full name, phone number, email address.
  - Service details, date, time slot, booking origin (Direct, AI Receptionist, Website).
  - Direct 1-tap "Call Client" (`tel:`) and "Send Email" (`mailto:`) action buttons.
  - Status progression buttons ("Confirm Appointment", "Cancel Booking").
• New Appointment BottomSheet Form:
  - Client full name (required), mobile phone (required), email (optional).
  - Service dropdown / text, date picker, time slot, internal notes.
  - Auto-saves with 'confirmed' status and updates dashboard metrics in real time.

[SECTION 3: CLIENT DIRECTORY (CustomersPage)]
• Search & Filter:
  - Search input matching customer name, phone number, email, or notes.
  - "With Email Only" filter toggle chip.
• Customer Cards & List:
  - Avatar monogram with initial letter.
  - Total appointment count badge & date of last visit.
  - Quick action buttons: Direct phone call & direct email outreach.
• Customer Details & Edit BottomSheet:
  - Full client profile view with historical bookings list.
  - Editable contact details and preferences notes.
• Add Client BottomSheet:
  - Quick manual intake form for walk-ins and phone calls.

[SECTION 4: INQUIRIES & TRANSCRIPTS (ChatInboxPage)]
• Navigation Behavior:
  - Always opens to the Inquiries Home List upon entering or re-entering the section.
• Dual-Pane Responsive Interface:
  - Mobile: Full-width conversation list. Tapping a dialogue smoothly transitions to
    the full message transcript with a top Back button (`ChevronLeft`).
  - Desktop: 4-column conversation session selector on the left, 8-column transcript on right.
• Transcript Viewer:
  - Read-only audit mode showing dialogue between AI Front Desk Agent and Web Visitor.
  - Distinct chat bubbles (Emerald bubble for AI Front Desk, Slate bubble for visitor).
  - Resulting Booking Banner: Links directly to scheduled appointment if the AI converted the chat.
  - Header contact bar with 1-tap Call and Email action buttons.

[SECTION 5: AI RECEPTIONIST CONFIG (AIReceptionistPage)]
• Master Controls:
  - Enable / Disable live front-desk widget toggle.
  - Tone of Voice selector (Professional, Warm, Friendly, Concise).
• Core Knowledge Base:
  - Business description, operating hours, physical location & parking notes.
  - Service booking instructions & deposit policies.
  - Information to collect from visitor (Name, phone, preferred time).
• Interactive FAQ Builder:
  - Dynamic list of question-and-answer pairs with add/delete controls.
• Safeguards & Boundary Rules:
  - "Do Not Claim" topics (medical advice, legal claims, off-topic promises).
  - Human escalation instructions.
• Live Interactive Test Playground:
  - Real-time conversational simulator with voice audio playback synthesizer.

[SECTION 6: WEBSITE BUILDER & PREVIEW (WebsitePage)]
• Device Viewport Simulation:
  - 1-click toggles between Desktop (100%), Tablet (768px), and Mobile (375px).
• Customization Controls:
  - Main headline, subheadline, and hero image selector / URL.
  - Call-to-action button text & top banner announcement.
• Deployment Pipeline:
  - Live deployment status indicator (Deployed / Live).
  - 1-click "Open Live Website" button to launch public customer-facing landing page.

[SECTION 7: AUTOMATIONS ENGINE (AutomationsPage)]
• Trigger Rules & Flows:
  - Instant Booking Confirmation SMS / Email (fires immediately upon booking).
  - 24-Hour Appointment Reminder (pre-visit reminder with cancellation rules).
  - Post-Service Follow-Up (solicits feedback & rebooking 24h after completion).
• Per-Rule Controls:
  - Enable/disable toggles, delay configuration, and message preview templates.
• Execution Log:
  - Real-time event monitor logging automated triggers.

[SECTION 8: EMAIL CENTER (EmailCenterPage)]
• Tabbed Navigation:
  - Compose Message: Recipient selector ('All Clients' or individual customer),
    subject line, rich body textarea, and send button.
  - Template Library: Pre-built & custom email templates with category tags,
    quick "Use Template" action, edit/delete actions, and "New Template" modal
    with dynamic tag helpers (`[Customer Name]`, `[Business Name]`, `[Service]`, etc.).
  - Recipients List: Directory of customers with valid email addresses and 1-tap
    "Compose to Client" shortcuts.
  - Delivery Log: Historical audit table tracking sent emails, recipients, and timestamps.
• Connected Email Domain Banner:
  - Displays authenticated business domain and delivery status.

[SECTION 9: SETTINGS & PREFERENCES (SettingsPage)]
• Business Profile:
  - Business name, phone, support email, address, city, timezone, and website URL.
• Social Media Integration:
  - Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps link, and Twitter/X profiles.
• Visual Theme Customizer:
  - Primary accent color swatch selector, corner radius (sm/md/lg/xl), and typography pairing.
• Feature Matrix Toggles:
  - Enable/disable Bookings, Chatbot, Email Center, and Automations modules.
• Multi-Tenant Code Management:
  - Display active Owner Code with copy button and switch tenant modal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MOBILE UI & TOUCH ERGONOMICS CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[✓] All buttons and clickable icons have minimum 44px x 44px tap targets.
[✓] Input fields use `h-10` / `h-11` with `px-3` and font size >= 16px to prevent iOS auto-zoom.
[✓] All modal dialogs on mobile reflow into full-width sliding BottomSheets with backdrop blur.
[✓] Bottom navigation bar has fixed positioning (`bottom-0 z-40`) with safe-area padding.
[✓] Main layout containers have `pb-24` on mobile to prevent content clipping under the nav bar.
[✓] Horizontal scrollbars for chips and tabs are styled with `overflow-x-auto scrollbar-none`.
[✓] Form actions have full-width primary submit buttons on mobile screens.
[✓] Auto-scrolls to top (`window.scrollTo(0, 0)`) on every section change and login.
[✓] Calming audio effects provide non-intrusive feedback without blocking user flow.
