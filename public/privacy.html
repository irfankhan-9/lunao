Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

Google API User Data Commitment

We only use Google user data to sign users in and don’t sell it. Lunao never uses, transfers, or shares your Google user profile data for advertising, data brokering, or model training.

1. Overview & Product Purpose

Lunao (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides an automated B2B local marketing and client acquisition platform. Our software allows agencies and business professionals to discover local businesses, generate customized preview websites, manage outreach via SMS and email, and review incoming booking requests.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal and operational data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we retain it, how you can request deletion, and how you can contact us.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide, secure, and operate the Lunao service:

Google Sign-In & Identity Information

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we collect your Google user ID, primary email address, full name, and avatar image. We use this strictly to authenticate your account and manage your workspace session. We only use Google user data to sign users in and don’t sell it.

Account & Campaign Configurations

Workspace settings, subscription tier, credit balances, outreach campaign configurations, customized template copy, and prospect records you import or create are stored securely in Supabase.

Communications Data (SMS & Email)

Outbound SMS messages sent via our Telnyx carrier integration, delivery logs, two-way conversational responses, and email outreach logs (recipient addresses and message bodies routed via Resend) are processed to provide campaign delivery tracking.

Technical & Hosting Metadata

When accessing Lunao or client websites, edge servers automatically log basic HTTP request metadata (IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and request timestamps) to protect infrastructure security and confirm edge health.

3. Why We Collect Information

We process data for the following specific purposes:

  • Authenticating user accounts and securing workspace sessions.
  • Generating, personalizing, and hosting live preview websites for local prospects.
  • Sending authorized SMS notifications through carrier 10DLC routes and dispatching outbound emails.
  • Recording incoming client appointment requests and syncing them to business owner PWAs.
  • Monitoring system performance, verifying edge server health (HTTP 200 checks), and preventing spam or abusive activity.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Subprocessors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information only to vetted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, subject to strict confidentiality and security obligations:

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

We retain your account profile, campaign history, and saved sites for as long as your workspace account is active or as necessary to provide you services. Operational logs and delivery metrics are retained for up to 90 days for network diagnostics, security monitoring, and carrier deliverability records, after which they are systematically purged.

You may request complete deletion of your account, personal data, and all associated prospect records at any time. To request deletion, email us at lunaoxx77@gmail.com. Deletion requests are processed and verified within 30 days.

6. Security Measures

We employ robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All web communications are encrypted via modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Database access requires authenticated tokens and row-level security (RLS).
